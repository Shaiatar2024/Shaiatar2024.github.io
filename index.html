My task board

I run several AI work sessions in parallel, so I built a Kanban board that keeps them in order. Every task has an ID, one line that says when it is done, and a log of what was decided. Each morning I know in 10 seconds what runs, what is stuck and what is next.

From question to launch

  1. 1

    Question. Every new piece of work starts with a round of hard questions, before any plan.

  2. 2

    Plan. Each project has a written work plan with phases: discovery, PRD, tech spec, plan, build, QA and ship.

  3. 3

    Hold the gate. Work that belongs to a later phase is refused until the earlier one is done.

  4. 4

    Delegate. Named specialist agents do the work, from research to security and release, and an orchestrator dispatches it.
